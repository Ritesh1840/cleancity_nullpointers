Waste problems linger because nobody sees them

Overflowing bins, plastic in drains and dumped rubble stay for days when there is no clear way to report them or find out what happened next.

One place to report, review and resolve

CleanCity stores every complaint with its photo and location, lets administrators update its status, and shows you each step as it happens.

How it works

  1. 1. Report the issue

    Describe the problem, add a photo and mark the location.

  2. 2. Authorities review it

    An administrator checks the report, sets its priority and adds remarks.

  3. 3. Track the resolution

    Follow the status from Submitted to Resolved in your dashboard.

Small habits keep waste off the street

Separate wet and dry waste. Food scraps in one bin; paper, plastic and metal in another.

Recycle clean. Rinse containers so they are not rejected at the sorting centre.

Dispose of electronics properly. Take old devices to an authorised e-waste collector.
